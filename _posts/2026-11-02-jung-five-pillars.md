---
title: Carl Jung’s 5 Pillars of a Happy Life
date: 2026-11-02 09:05:00 +0530
---

1. Good physical and mental health.
2. Good personal and intimate relationships, such as marriage, family, and friendships.
3. The faculty for perceiving beauty in art and nature.
4. Reasonable standards of living and satisfactory work.
5. A philosophical or religious point of view capable of coping successfully with the vicissitudes of life.
